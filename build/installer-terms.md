# Freevy

Freevy is free software under the GNU General Public License v3. Source code: https://github.com/FreevyApp/freevy

## Privacy

- Freevy watches your typing on this PC only to find your abbreviations. Typed text is never stored or sent anywhere.
- Your snippets stay on this PC. They are synced only if you sign in to a Freevy account, and then end-to-end encrypted: nobody but you can read them.
- A few times a day Freevy asks freevy.app for a new version. The request carries your Freevy version and Windows architecture, nothing else about you. Turn it off under Settings, "Update automatically".
- No telemetry, no crash reports, no advertising.

Full privacy policy: https://freevy.app/privacy

## What the installer changes

Freevy installs for your Windows user only, without admin rights. It adds Start menu and desktop shortcuts, starts with Windows (Settings, "Start with Windows") and handles freevy:// links for signing in. Uninstall it from Windows Settings, Apps; your snippets in %APPDATA%\Freevy are kept.
